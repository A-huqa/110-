1:05The Story

Why 1:10?

A clock is the only thing that treats everyone the same. It does not care where you were born, what you drive, or who your father knows. Everybody gets twenty-four hours. What separates people is what they do inside them.

One o'clock is when you decide. Ten past one is when you have already moved — while the rest of the room is still deciding. That ten-minute gap is the whole brand.

Ten minutes past the hour is already late for anyone still waiting.

We cut and sew in Nairobi, with Nairobi tailors, for people who are building something here. Two hundred pieces at a time, four times a year. When they're gone, they're gone.

    When exactly does Drop 001 open?

    10 January 2026 at 1:10 PM East Africa Time. The product images on this page unblur automatically at that moment — you don't need to refresh. Waitlist members get access from 12:40 PM.

    How many pieces are there?

    200 in total: 110 tees and 90 fitted caps. There is no restock of Drop 001. The next drop lands in April 2026.

    Can I pay with M-Pesa?

    Yes. M-Pesa is the default at checkout, alongside Visa and Mastercard. You'll get an STK push to the number on your order.

    Where do you ship?

    Nairobi delivery in 1–2 days. Everywhere else in Kenya, 2–4 days by courier. Pickup from our January pop-up is free — pick that option at checkout.

    What's the sizing like?

    The tee is a boxy, slightly oversized fit at 240 GSM. Caps are structured six-panel, one size, adjustable. Full measurements go live with the drop.

    Does joining the waitlist mean I've bought something?

    No. The waitlist reserves your early access window and holds your size for one hour after the drop opens. No payment is taken until you check out.
